# Tasalli (تسلّی)
### An AI companion grounded in the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah

> *"Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor has He become displeased."* (Ad-Duha 93:3)

**Working name:** Tasalli: consolation, reassurance
**Status:** Idea / Phase 1 planning
**Date:** 25 October 2026

---

## 1. The Idea in One Line

A gentle Muslim companion you can talk to (by chat, and later by voice) when you're worried, sad, or stuck. It listens first, then consoles and guides you with **verified** Qur'an, authentic hadith, and duas. Over time it helps you grow as a Muslim through caring reminders about salah, dua, and your responsibilities to family.

## 2. The Name

- **Tasalli (تسلّی)** means consolation or reassurance. In Urdu, *"tasalli rakho"* means "don't worry, be at ease."
- **Arabic root:** *tasliyah*, consoling. Scholars use this term for the verses Allah revealed to console the Prophet ﷺ in difficult times, such as **Surah Ad-Duha** and **Surah Ash-Sharh**.
- **Why it works:**
  - It describes exactly what the app does.
  - It's easy to say in English, Urdu, and voice mode.
  - It's distinctive enough to own.
- **Taglines:**
  - *"Your Lord has not forsaken you."* (93:3)
  - *"Indeed, with hardship comes ease."* (94:5–6)

**Names considered and rejected:**
- Miraj: taken by an established kids' app.
- Momin: very crowded, and one existing app is sectarian.
- Habibi: romantic or casual feel.
- Sahib: means "sir" or "master" in Urdu.
- Sakinah: 6+ products already, including a near-identical Android app.
- Thabat and Hamdam: both taken.

## 3. Core Promise

> **"A companion who listens first, then reminds you of what Allah and His Messenger ﷺ said, with every reference real and checkable."**

Authenticity is the product. An AI that invents a hadith, misquotes an ayah, or attributes a weak story to the Prophet ﷺ causes real religious harm. Tasalli's main advantage over generic chatbots is that **every citation can be trusted.**

## 4. Thinking Like the User

### Who will use it

| Persona | Situation | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| The worried professional | Afraid of losing a job, job hunting, money stress | Practical steps, tawakkul, duas for rizq |
| The grieving | Loss of a parent, child, or friend | Patience, hope, duas for the deceased |
| The anxious student | Exams, future uncertainty | Calm, focus, duas for ease |
| The struggling believer | Feels far from Allah, missed prayers, guilt | Mercy, tawbah, a gentle way back without judgement |
| The overwhelmed parent or spouse | Family conflict, responsibility pressure | Patience, family rights, practical advice |
| The new Muslim or revert | Learning basics, feeling alone | Simple guidance, community pointers |

### Example journey: User 1, worried about his job

**User:** *Assalam o Alaikum, I'm worried about my job. What should I do?*

**Tasalli:**
- Replies to the salam warmly and acknowledges the worry.
- Asks one or two questions: *Have you lost the job, or are you afraid of losing it? What steps have you taken so far?*

**After the user answers, Tasalli responds in two parts.**

1. **Practical help:** concrete next steps, such as updating the CV, networking, applying daily, and upskilling.
2. **Spiritual grounding (always with sources):**
   - *"Tie your camel and trust in Allah."* (Tirmidhi 2517, hasan). Do your part, then leave the outcome to Allah.
   - *"If you relied on Allah as He truly should be relied upon, He would provide for you as He provides for the birds…"* (Tirmidhi 2344)
   - Qur'an **65:2–3**: provision from where you do not expect.
   - Qur'an **94:5–6**: with hardship comes ease.
   - **The dua:** *Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan…* ("O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief…"), Sahih al-Bukhari 6369. Shown in Arabic, with transliteration, translation, and recorded audio.

**Days later, Tasalli follows up:**
*"How did the interview go? Have you been able to keep reading that dua?"*

> **Note on authenticity in practice:** the well-known story of the man in the masjid overwhelmed by debt, whom the Prophet ﷺ taught this dua, is in Abu Dawud and graded **weak** by many scholars, while the dua itself is **authentic** in Bukhari. Tasalli quotes the authentic version, and either omits the weak story or labels it clearly. This is the standard everywhere in the app.

## 5. Conversation Principles

1. **Listen before advising.** Empathy first, and never open with a lecture.
2. **Practical and spiritual together.** Effort plus tawakkul, as the Sunnah teaches.
3. **Only verified sources.** Quote only from the curated library, never from the model's memory.
4. **Every citation is tappable.** It opens the full Arabic text, the book and number, and the grade.
5. **No fatwas.** Fiqh rulings, divorce, inheritance, and complex personal matters are referred to a qualified scholar.
6. **No spiritual blame.** Never say "you're suffering because your iman is weak."
7. **A friend, not a policeman.** Mercy over guilt, especially with someone who has drifted.
8. **Not a replacement for people.** Encourage family, community, the masjid, and professionals.

## 6. Features

### Phase 1 (MVP)

- **Chat in English** that is salam-aware, warm, and concise.
- **12–15 life situations** with curated texts:
  - Job and rizq
  - Money and debt
  - Anxiety
  - Grief
  - Illness
  - Exams
  - Marriage
  - Family conflict
  - Guilt and tawbah
  - Loneliness
  - Anger
  - Gratitude
  - Patience in hardship
- **A verified citation card** for every quote: Arabic, translation, reference, grade, and a link to read more.
- **Gentle prayer check-ins.** Example: *"Asr time. Have you managed to pray?"* with answers Yes / Will do now / Not today, and a merciful response either way.
- **Morning and evening adhkar reminders** with audio.
- **A personal dua plan.** The user's current situation produces this week's duas, and Tasalli follows up on them.
- **Crisis safety routing** (see section 9).
- **Privacy-first settings.** Every reminder is opt-in, and the user controls how strict their companion is.

### Phase 2

- **Voice mode:** speech-to-text in, warm text-to-speech out. **Arabic Qur'an and duas always use recorded recitation, never TTS.**
- **Urdu and Roman Urdu**, using established Urdu translations rather than machine translation.
- **A responsibilities reflection**, based on *"Each of you is a shepherd…"* (Bukhari). A weekly prompt asks whether you called your parents, spent time with your children, or gave sadaqah. The user picks which areas they want to be held accountable for.
- **A gratitude and reflection journal.**
- **Seasonal modes** for Ramadan, Dhul Hijjah, and Jumu'ah.
- **Expanded library** toward the full hadith corpus, with grading filters.

### Phase 3 (ideas)

- Arabic and other languages
- Local masjid and scholar directory
- Family or circle accountability (opt-in)
- Optional "ask a scholar" escalation partnership

## 7. Data Sources (Free and Authentic)

### Key decision: curate, don't dump

Phase 1 uses a **hand-verified library of about 300–500 texts** (ayat, sahih/hasan hadith, and duas) tagged by situation. The AI retrieves only from this library.

- **Safer:** every text has been checked by a human.
- **Reviewable:** a scholar can realistically check 400 items.
- **More relevant:** the AI gets better material than from a huge raw database.

### Sources

| Need | Source | License / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Qur'an, Arabic text** | [Tanzil.net](https://tanzil.net) | CC BY 3.0. Verbatim only (**no changes allowed**). Must credit Tanzil and link to tanzil.net |
| **Qur'an, translations** | [QuranEnc.com](https://quranenc.com) (incl. Urdu), Tanzil translations | Tanzil translations are **non-commercial only**; commercial use needs the translator's or publisher's permission. Check QuranEnc terms |
| **Qur'an, API, audio, tafsir** | [Quran.com / Quran.Foundation API](https://api-docs.quran.foundation) | Free access for Islamic apps. Use it for recitation audio |
| **Hadith, Arabic and grades** | [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) | Public domain / Unlicense. Covers the 6 books plus Muwatta (~36k hadith), with grades |
| **Hadith, cleaned with licence flags** | [quranlab/hadith (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/quranlab/hadith) | Multi-grader grades (al-Albani, Ahmad Shakir, Zubair Ali Zai…). `license`, `attribution` and `commercial_ok` per row |
| **Hadith, reference and cross-check** | [Sunnah.com API](https://sunnah.com/developers) | API key requested via a GitHub issue (**apply early**). English translations are copyrighted, so don't republish without permission |
| **Duas** | Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) | Several open JSON/API versions on GitHub. Verify each against the book and check its grading |
| **Prayer times** | Adhan library (JS / Dart / Swift) | Open source, calculated on-device, so no location is sent to the server |
| **Recitation audio** | Quran.com / EveryAyah-style recitation sources | Check licence per reciter |

> ⚠️ **Translations are where copyright usually sits**, even when the Arabic text is public domain. Filter by `commercial_ok`, get permission, or write short translations in-house and have a scholar review them.

### Library record format (one row per text)

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| id | `rizq-007` |
| situation_tags | job, rizq, anxiety |

## 8. Technical Architecture (Phase 1)

```
User (Flutter / React Native app)
        │
        ▼
API backend (Supabase / Node)
        │
        ├── Safety layer ── crisis detection → helplines and caring response
        │
        ├── Retrieval ── pgvector search over the curated library
        │                  (by situation and meaning)
        │
        ├── LLM (Claude or similar)
        │     System prompt: "Only quote texts passed to you.
        │     Never quote from memory. Always include the reference."
        │
        └── Citation validator ── every reference in the reply must
              exist in the DB; if not → block or regenerate
        │
        ▼
Reply + tappable citation cards
```

| Layer | Choice |
|---|---|
| Mobile | Flutter or React Native (one codebase for Android and iOS) |
| Backend / DB | Supabase / Postgres plus **pgvector** |
| LLM | Claude (or similar) via API |
| Prayer times | Adhan library, on-device |
| Notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging / APNs |
| Voice (Phase 2) | Speech-to-text plus TTS for English/Urdu, recorded audio for Arabic |

**The citation validator is the most important piece of code in the app.** It is what prevents fake hadith from reaching users.

## 9. Safety, Ethics and Privacy

### Mental health and crisis
- If a user signals self-harm or suicidal thoughts, Tasalli leaves "dua mode":
  - responds with care,
  - shares crisis helplines for the user's country,
  - encourages contacting a trusted person.
- Faith is never presented as a replacement for professional help. Islam encourages seeking treatment.
- The app is not therapy, and onboarding says so clearly.

### Religious integrity
- Every text comes from the curated library, with grading.
- No fatwas; fiqh questions are referred to scholars.
- Avoid sectarian framing so the app welcomes all Muslims.
- **A scholar advisor** reviews the library and the bot's tone. This is also a key trust signal for marketing.

### Privacy (GDPR, launching from Germany/EU)
- Religious practice and emotional state count as **special category data (GDPR Art. 9)** and need explicit consent and strong protection.
- Minimise the data stored. Keep prayer and adhkar logs **on-device** where possible.
- Encrypt in transit and at rest. Let users delete everything with one tap.
- **No ads, ever** — especially none based on someone's sadness or emotional state.
- Conversation logs are reviewed only with explicit opt-in consent.

## 12. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI invents or misattributes hadith | Retrieval-only design, citation validator, curated and graded library |
| Weak hadith presented as authentic | Grade stored per text; weak narrations excluded or clearly labelled |
| User in crisis | Detection, helplines, caring escalation |
| Feels judgemental or naggy | Opt-in reminders, mercy-first tone, user-controlled strictness |
| Sectarian backlash | Neutral sources, scholar review, no fiqh rulings |
| Copyright on translations | Licence filtering, permissions, scholar-reviewed in-house translations |
| Privacy breach of sensitive data | On-device storage, encryption, GDPR Art. 9 compliance, data minimisation |
| Emotional dependency on the AI | Encourage real relationships, masjid, family, and professionals |
